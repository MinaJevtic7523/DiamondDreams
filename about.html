About us

Diamond Dreams is a fictional jewelry store created as a web design project. It shows how a small shop could present its watches, necklaces, bracelets, rings and earrings online.

The idea behind the brand is simple: timeless elegance, honest craftsmanship and friendly service. Explore the collection and imagine which piece would complete your style.

The site was designed and built by Mina Jevtić with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
